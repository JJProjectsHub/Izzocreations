Hi, we're Izzo!

We are two sisters who absolutely love making things. We started Izzo because we wanted to share our handmade creations with people in our local community — and hopefully a bit further too!

Every single item in our shop is made by us, by hand, with love and care. No two pieces are exactly the same, which makes each one really special.

We make jewellery, bracelets, keyrings and bookmarks — and we love making custom orders too, so if you have an idea, just ask!

We give back 🌍

We believe in doing good while making beautiful things. That's why we donate 35% of every sale to WWF to help protect wildlife around the world.

Why handmade?

We believe handmade things are extra special because someone put real time and care into making them. When you buy something from Izzo, you know it was made just for you — not by a machine in a factory, but by us, at home, with our own hands. That means a lot to us, and we hope it means something to you too.
