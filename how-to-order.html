How to Order

Ordering from Izzo is really simple — just follow these easy steps!

It's as easy as 1, 2, 3

1

Choose what you'd like

Browse our Shop and pick the items you love. Note down the names so you can include them in your email.

2

Email us

Send an email to izzohandmade2@gmail.com with the items you'd like and any special requests. We'll reply with the total price.

3

Pay and receive

Once we've agreed on everything, you can pay by bank transfer or cash. Then we'll get making and arrange delivery or collection!
