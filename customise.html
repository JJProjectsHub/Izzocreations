Make it yours

We love creating something truly unique, just for you. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll make it happen!

How it works

Want a bracelet in your favourite colours? A necklace with a special charm? A keyring as a gift for someone special? Just ask us — we love a challenge!

Simply email us at izzohandmade2@gmail.com and tell us what you'd like. We'll chat about colours, styles and sizes, agree a price, and get making!
